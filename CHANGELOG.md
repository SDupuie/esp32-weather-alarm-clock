## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-21

**Weather alarm clock for the Waveshare ESP32-P4**

### Features

- Analog and digital clock faces with Day and Night appearances.
- One-time and repeating alarms with sound previews, Snooze, Slide to Dismiss and restart recovery.
- Sound Machine with nine looping recordings, three generated noises, independent volume and sleep timers.
- Current weather and five-day forecasts from Open-Meteo or Visual Crossing.
- Full-screen U.S. Radar with a 75-mile coverage diameter and six-frame playback, plus official NWS watches and warnings for covered locations.
- Night Shift with fixed or sunset-to-sunrise scheduling, separate Day/Night brightness and gradual transitions.
- Optional battery-backed DS3231 clock for timekeeping through power interruptions.
- Touchscreen settings, Wi-Fi setup and household messaging from a phone or computer.
- Chrome installation, source-build instructions and printable Orbit enclosure files.

Inspired by [Round Weather Display](https://github.com/Canterrain/round-weather-display) by Josh Hendrickson.

See the [user guide](README.md) for current features and source credits. Product behavior is defined in [the architecture](docs/architecture.md).
