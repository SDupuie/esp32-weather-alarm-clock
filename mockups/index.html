Round Weather Display

Visual mockups of the current ESP32-P4 application. Each round screen uses an 800 × 800 canvas, the firmware’s decoded artwork, its Montserrat font, and positions from app_ui.c. Time, weather, networks, and messages are illustrative sample data. These are browser reconstructions, not captures from the hardware; text rasterization and the default LVGL setup controls may differ slightly.
